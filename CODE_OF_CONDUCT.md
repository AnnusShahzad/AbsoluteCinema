# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and welcoming, including toward people with different viewpoints and experience levels.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its community.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer, [@AnnusShahzad](https://github.com/AnnusShahzad), through GitHub. All reports are reviewed promptly and handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, and temporarily or permanently ban contributors, for behaviour that violates this code.

The full text of the Contributor Covenant, including the enforcement guidelines, is available at <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.
